This Is Not
Standard Affiliate.

Kibeeri is a performance infrastructure platform. The distinction is structural — not cosmetic. Six pillars define the difference.

Six Pillars

The Kibeeri Advantage

These are not marketing claims. They are structural properties of the platform that differentiate Kibeeri from the broader affiliate publishing market.

Pillar 01
Performance Infrastructure

Kibeeri is not a blog with affiliate links. It is a purpose-built infrastructure: 46 domains, 50M+ ranked pages, AI-driven ranking engine, and GEO-specific conversion optimisation. The scale and engineering investment required to replicate this is measured in years, not months.

  • 46 active domains across 10 GEO markets
  • 50M+ indexed, ranked comparison pages
  • Continuous algorithmic updates — not editorial cycles
Pillar 02
Search Intent Dominance

Kibeeri targets decision-stage search intent — the queries where shoppers are comparing specific products before purchase. This is the highest-value segment of the purchase funnel. It is also the most technically demanding to capture at scale, which is why most publishers operate only at the awareness or consideration stages.

  • Decision-stage query targeting exclusively
  • Pre-qualified traffic — intent established before click
  • +25% conversion lift documented versus direct traffic
Pillar 03
Long-Tail Coverage

Broad keyword traffic is commoditised. Conversion rates are low and competition is high. Kibeeri operates in the long tail — millions of specific comparison queries with high intent and lower competitive pressure. This is where the most valuable traffic lives, and where Kibeeri's infrastructure holds the most dominant positions.

  • 20M+ active comparison chart pages
  • Millions of distinct long-tail comparison queries covered
  • Lower competition, higher conversion per query
Pillar 04
Algorithmic Exposure Allocation

Product placement is determined by a multi-signal ranking algorithm — not by editorial relationships or manual curation. Reviews, price, availability, and conversion history determine position. Commission tier advancement unlocks prioritised allocation. The system rewards performance, not relationships.

  • AI-driven ranking across 5 performance dimensions
  • Real-time price and availability signal integration
  • Commission tiers unlock compounding placement advantages
Pillar 05
Zero Upfront Capital Risk

The CPS model eliminates advertiser risk entirely. There is no media budget to allocate. No impression cost to carry. No campaign spend to justify. Commission is paid on Amazon-confirmed sales only — meaning the advertiser's unit economics remain intact regardless of traffic volume. Scale is the only variable.

  • Commission activates on OPS-confirmed Amazon sale only
  • No minimum spend, no setup fees, no campaign cost
  • Financial risk profile identical to in-house sales
Pillar 06
Full Amazon Programme Compliance

All Kibeeri operations are conducted within Amazon Associates programme terms. Attribution is clean. Tracking is standard. There is no cookie stuffing, no forced redirect, no terms circumvention of any kind. Compliance is not a marketing point — it is an operational requirement that underpins the entire attribution chain.

  • Amazon Associates programme terms adhered to in all GEOs
  • Standard attribution — no tracking manipulation
  • Full compliance documentation available on request
