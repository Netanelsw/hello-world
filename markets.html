Market Architecture

Each GEO is a Standalone Infrastructure

Market presence is not a translated version of another GEO. It is a fully independent comparison stack built for each country's search behavior, product catalog, and Amazon programme.

Dedicated Local Domains

Each GEO operates on country-code top-level domains or market-specific domain strategies. Local TLDs signal GEO relevance to both search engines and shoppers.

Native Language Content

All comparison content is native language — not machine-translated. Local editorial standards maintained per market to ensure ranking quality and user trust.

GEO-Specific Ranking

Ranking signals are calibrated per market. UK shopper behavior differs from JP — review weight, price sensitivity, and category prioritisation are tuned accordingly.
