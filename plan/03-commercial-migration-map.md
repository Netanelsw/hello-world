## 3.3 CPC pricing method (anchor for every CPC row)
`CPC = historical commissions ÷ historical clicks` (Dec 2025–Feb 2026 baseline, ~$0.92 avg).
Anomalous ASINs (low clicks/revenue) → replaced with portfolio average. Validate monthly:
`clicks × CPC = fee`.
