- [ ] Pull margin per page vs. old blended baseline

- **Pass (3) → expand MeLi product cohort; gate Bol behind MeLi being consistently positive**
- **Fail (2) → deprioritize agency track (Test 4 loses its measurement rail)**

### Week 8 — Test 4 decision
- [ ] Pull gross margin on brand engagement

- **Pass → post job for dedicated agency/account lead; define scope from engagement learnings**
- **Fail → do not build the agency track; revert Netanel capacity to Tests 1–3 scale work**
