# 05 — Test 4: Brand-Direct / Agency

**Pass bar:** One brand's full marketing funnel runs at gross margin ≥ current affiliate contribution margin, repeatably.
**Decide by:** Week 8.
**Owners:** Netanel (define + land deal #1) → dedicated hire (execute + scale after proof).
**Dev needed:** Minimal for the test. Infrastructure investment comes post-proof.

---

## What this test proves

That Kibeeri can own a brand's full marketing funnel — not just drive traffic — and charge for it at economics that beat the affiliate model. This is the highest-ceiling track and the furthest from current operations. It's also the only one that fully escapes Amazon's control.

The test is deliberately small: land one brand, run their full funnel for 6–8 weeks, prove the margin. Don't build an agency before the unit economics are real.

---

## 5.1 What "full funnel" means here

Not a rebrand of the existing affiliate link model. Full funnel means:
- Kibeeri owns the campaign strategy, creative direction, media buying, and optimization
- Traffic drives to the brand's own site or Amazon listing (via Attribution — see Test 2)
- Kibeeri is paid a management fee + performance component (not a commission on Amazon sales)
- Kibeeri owns the reporting relationship — not Amazon, not the affiliate program

This is why Test 2 (Attribution) is a dependency: Attribution gives us the measurement rail that makes running a brand's full Amazon funnel defensible and billable. Attribution-based reporting is what we sell.

---

## 5.2 The test setup

**Step 1 — Define the product clearly.**
Before outreach, write a one-page description of what you're selling:
- What we manage: paid media (PPC + social) driving to brand's Amazon listing via Attribution
- What we charge: [retainer] + [performance component — TBD, e.g., % of attributed GMV above baseline]
- What we deliver: weekly Attribution-based reporting at ASIN/campaign level
- What we don't do: Associates-model affiliate (that's a different track)

- [ ] Write the one-pager `[Netanel]`

**Step 2 — Identify candidate brand.**
Criteria:
- Already a Kibeeri partner or warm relationship (fastest path to yes)
- US brand with active Amazon presence
- Willing to pay a management fee, not just CPS
- Category where our traffic/content capabilities are strong

- [ ] Identify 3–5 candidates `[Netanel + Mark G]`
- [ ] Pitch: "We run your full Amazon marketing funnel — media buying, optimization, Attribution reporting. You pay us a management fee. You own the results." `[Netanel]`
- [ ] Land 1 signed deal

**Step 3 — Run the engagement.**
- [ ] Set up Attribution (Test 2 infrastructure) for this brand `[Daniel]`
- [ ] Run media buying for 6–8 weeks `[Shaked]`
- [ ] Weekly reporting to brand `[Netanel or Mark G]`
- [ ] Track: gross margin on the engagement (fee received minus media spend minus Netanel/Mark G time)

---

## 5.3 What to measure

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Deal landed | 1 signed engagement by Wk 4 |
| Gross margin on engagement | ≥ affiliate contribution margin on equivalent traffic |
| Brand satisfaction / renewal signal | Positive — would they renew or expand? |
| Time cost (Netanel hours) | Quantify — this determines whether you need a dedicated hire immediately |

---

## 5.4 Pass / fail

**Pass:** Margin parity or better, brand wants to continue, and the model is repeatable without Netanel doing all the delivery work.
→ Hire a dedicated account/agency lead. Build out the product. This becomes the primary long-term revenue engine.

**Fail — margin:** Economics don't work at the engagement fee brands will pay vs. our media costs.
→ Reprice or reposition. One retry with a different fee structure before writing off. This could also mean the product needs Attribution (Test 2) to mature first.

**Fail — capacity:** Test 2 (Attribution) isn't ready in time, or Netanel's time cost is too high to run the engagement alongside other responsibilities.
→ Delay Test 4 until Attribution is proven. Don't run the agency test without the measurement infrastructure — it's not credible without it.

---

## 5.5 The hire

After proof: the dedicated hire is an account/agency lead who can own brand relationships end-to-end — not a salesperson, someone who can also manage delivery. This is not Mark G's role (Mark G stays on partnerships and Attribution brand sales). The hire activates only after Test 4 passes.

Do not pre-hire. The test tells you whether the role is needed and what it should look like.
