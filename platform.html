Each page is structured around a specific comparison query: product vs product, category vs category, price point vs price point. The intent is pre-qualified before any traffic reaches a brand's product page.

Layer 02

Ranking Engine

Product placement is determined algorithmically — not manually. The engine continuously evaluates products across five performance dimensions and adjusts position in real time.

01
Reviews & Ratings

Aggregate rating score, review volume, and recency. Weighted to surface products with demonstrated buyer satisfaction.

02
Price Intelligence

Real-time price monitoring against category benchmarks. Competitively priced products receive higher exposure in price-sensitive queries.

03
Availability Status

In-stock signals updated continuously. Out-of-stock products are deprioritised automatically to protect conversion quality.

04
Click-to-Convert Performance

Historical conversion signals per product, per GEO, per category. The engine learns which products convert and surfaces them accordingly.

05
Commission Tier

Advertisers who advance to higher commission tiers unlock premium Top 1–3 placement across relevant charts. Scaled commission unlocks scaled exposure.
