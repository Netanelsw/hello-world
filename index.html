Value Generation Engine

How We Generate Value

Five integrated layers convert passive search activity into qualified purchase events — each optimised and measured independently.

Kibeeri's infrastructure begins where purchase decisions begin — in search. Our proprietary keyword mapping system tracks 50M+ long-tail product queries across 46 domains and 11 languages, identifying buyer-intent signals at the lowest possible level of the funnel.

Each query is classified by intent strength, category affinity, and conversion probability before a single page is indexed. This allows the platform to prioritise traffic sources where the probability of a confirmed purchase is statistically highest.

50M+ tracked queries Intent classification 46 domains 11 languages

Product placement across 20M+ comparison charts is determined by a multi-variable ranking model that incorporates commission tier, historical conversion rate, category relevance, and seasonal demand signals.

The model is updated continuously. A product appearing in position 3 today may move to position 1 tomorrow based on real-time performance data — and vice versa. Commission tier is a direct input into ranking weight, creating a measurable and transparent scaling mechanism for brands.

20M+ comparison charts Multi-variable ranking Real-time updates Commission-weighted

Each comparison page is architecturally designed to reduce friction in the decision process. Product specifications, price history, review aggregations, and feature comparisons are rendered dynamically — optimised for the specific device, GEO, and buyer context detected at the session level.

Kibeeri's click-through and add-to-cart rates consistently outperform industry benchmarks because the pages serve buyers already in the evaluation phase, not discovery.

Dynamic rendering GEO-specific content Device optimisation High-intent traffic only

For brands at Scale tier and above, Kibeeri allocates PPC amplification budget to extend the reach of top-performing product pages. This creates a compounding effect: organic ranking performance is supplemented by targeted paid distribution — both measured against the same CPS metric.

Seasonal boost allocations are also managed at this layer, ensuring brands maintain visibility during peak demand windows (Prime Day, Black Friday, category-specific peaks) without requiring separate campaign management overhead.

PPC amplification Seasonal boost allocation Compounding visibility Scale tier+

All traffic, click, and conversion events are tracked at the order level with GMT-timestamped OPS data. Brands receive proforma reporting during the confirmation window, followed by confirmed invoice issuance once Amazon validates the underlying order data.

The reporting cadence is calibrated to each tier: monthly for Starter, bi-weekly for Growth, weekly for Scale and above. For Strategic partners, real-time dashboard access is available with full SKU-level and GEO-level breakdown.
