The Model

Cost-Per-Sale. Nothing Else.

The Kibeeri model is structurally aligned with advertiser outcomes. Commission is paid on confirmed sales only. There is no fee for impressions, clicks, placements, or page rankings.

This alignment is intentional. Kibeeri's infrastructure is only commercially viable if it drives verified purchases. The incentive structure is identical to the advertiser's — every commission paid represents a sale made.

Key Commercial Terms
  • Commission rate agreed per category and per GEO
  • No minimum spend commitment
  • No impression or click fees at any point
  • Commission activates on OPS-confirmed Amazon sale only
  • Proforma reviewed by partner before final invoice issuance

Process

How Partnership Works

From initial inquiry to active partner status. Straightforward onboarding, clear terms, no ambiguity.

01
Inquiry & Qualification

Submit a partner inquiry including company, market of interest, and monthly Amazon sales volume. Kibeeri reviews fit against current category availability and GEO infrastructure capacity.

02
Commercial Agreement

Commission rate agreed per category and per GEO. No minimums. No setup fees. Agreement covers attribution methodology, OPS reporting cadence, and invoice process.

03
Product Integration

Partner products are ingested into the Kibeeri ranking engine. ASIN-level data, pricing, and availability feeds are configured. Integration is completed within the agreed onboarding window.

04
Go Live & OPS Monitoring

Products become active across qualifying comparison charts. OPS tracking confirms attribution from day one. Monthly reporting begins immediately.

05
Tier Advancement

As commission volume grows, partners advance through tiers. Higher tiers unlock premium placement, dedicated charts, PPC amplification, and cross-GEO expansion priority.

Onboarding Requirements
  • Active Amazon Associates account (or programme eligibility)
  • Product ASINs for target GEO markets
  • Commission rate agreement per category
  • Designated commercial contact for reporting
What Is Not Required
  • Upfront media budget or campaign spend
  • Creative assets, banners, or ad materials
  • Minimum sales guarantee or volume commitment
  • Dedicated tracking pixels or custom integrations
Reporting

OPS-Backed Reporting. No Estimates.

All performance data is sourced directly from Amazon's OPS reporting system. Nothing is modelled, projected, or estimated. Every figure is Amazon-confirmed.

01
Monthly OPS Delivery

Full Amazon OPS export delivered monthly per GEO. Includes click volume, conversion rate, attributed orders, and gross sales value.

02
Proforma Review

Commission proforma generated from OPS data. Sent to partner for review and approval before invoice issuance. No surprises. No retroactive adjustments.

03
Confirmed Invoice

Final invoice issued post-approval. Standard net payment terms. Full reconciliation documentation included. Dispute resolution process defined in commercial agreement.
